What is Neura-Mitram?

In plain English, no tech jargon: it's a private place to say what's actually on your mind — and get an honest answer back.

Think of it like a journal that talks back. You speak or type whatever you're feeling. It figures out what's really going on underneath, tells you the truth about it, and gives you one small thing you can do right now. No judgment, no waiting for a friend to reply.
